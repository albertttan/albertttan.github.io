Common Questions & Answers

Most UK universities accept applications through UCAS starting in September for courses beginning the following September or October. Key deadline for most courses is January 15th, though Oxbridge and medicine/dentistry deadlines are October 15th. It's best to start preparing your application in the summer before your final year.

If you have settled or pre-settled status under the Ukraine schemes, you may be eligible for home fee status and student finance (tuition fee loans and maintenance loans). Many universities also offer specific scholarships for Ukrainian students. Additionally, there are charitable organizations providing support.

Yes! Your school or college should provide support. Additionally, many Ukrainian community organizations in the UK offer mentoring. The UCAS website has extensive guidance, and some universities offer pre-application workshops. Remember to be authentic and share your unique perspective and experiences.
